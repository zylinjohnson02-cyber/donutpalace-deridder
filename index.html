Before sunrise on Pine Street

Donut Palace opens at 4:30 in the morning Monday through Saturday, and at 5 on Sunday. The glass case holds glazed and chocolate-iced donuts, cake donuts, twists, eclairs and apple fritters, with kolaches, croissant sandwiches, biscuits, burritos and boudin for something savory.

Grab a coffee or a cappuccino, take a table inside, or take a box to go.
